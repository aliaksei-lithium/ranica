Gemini 4: strong model, nervous investors

Google's new model leads most public benchmarks and ships with a 2-million-token context window and a cheaper 'Flash' tier. The market read the launch differently: Alphabet guided to another year of record data-centre spending and gave no new revenue figure for AI products, and the shares fell 10 % in a day. The three numbers analysts watched: capex, cloud growth and the share of searches answered by AI overviews.The Verge · 9 min

Kotlin 2.4 and the new incremental compiler

The compiler now tracks dependencies per declaration instead of per file, so changing a private function no longer rebuilds everything that imports the class. JetBrains quotes 30–40 % shorter rebuilds on a 2 000-module Android project; a changed public signature still forces a wider rebuild. K2 is the only frontend now, and the old one is removed.JetBrains blog · 6 min

Germany
Heating law: what the amendment actually changes

The 2024 Building Energy Act set dates after which a new heating system must run on 65 % renewable energy. The amendment pushes those dates back by up to four years for older buildings, widens the exceptions for owners over 70, and keeps the subsidy of up to 70 % for heat pumps. Tenants keep the existing cap on rent increases after a modernisation.Deutschlandfunk · 7 min
